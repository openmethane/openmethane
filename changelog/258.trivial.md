Added `src/analysis/aerosol_bias/`, diagnostics that test whether the observed column's dependence on retrieved aerosol scales with the length of the light path, for [#249](https://github.com/openmethane/openmethane/issues/249), along with two analysis utilities they build on: `sounding_cache.py`, which flattens an archived monthly run's observations into per-sounding arrays, and `sounding_geometry.py`, which reconstructs the solar and viewing zenith angles the observation files drop. None of them are part of a normal run.
