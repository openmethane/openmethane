Added `src/analysis/aerosol_bias/`, diagnostics that test whether the observed column's dependence on retrieved aerosol scales with the length of the light path, for [#249](https://github.com/openmethane/openmethane/issues/249). The per-sounding cache they read now carries the solar and viewing geometry, reconstructed from each sounding's timestamp and pixel corners; neither is part of a normal run.
