Find TropOMI granules with the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem catalogue instead
of listing every granule for each day and discarding the ones that miss the
bounding box.

The catalogue matches on each granule's swath footprint, so only granules that
cross the box are downloaded. For the `aust` domain that is around 4 granules a
day rather than 15, cutting the daily download from roughly 950 MB to 250 MB.
Searching needs no credentials.

Whichever product the catalogue holds for a date is now fetched. It keeps a single
current product per orbit and deletes superseded ones, so granules up to
2022-07-25 come from ESA's full mission reprocessing at processor version
02.04.00, rather than the superseded offline products at 02.03.01 and earlier that
listing the bucket returned. Near real time products are excluded, and the fetch
fails if the catalogue ever returns two products for one orbit, either of which
would duplicate observations.
