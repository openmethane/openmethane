Changed the inversion's boundary condition term from an emission flux at the domain edge into a correction to the boundary condition itself.

- Previously, the `bcon` group of the prior and posterior files was a flux in ppm/s added to the ring of cells at the domain edge, over 8 fixed regions (4 edges by 2 layer bands) and a single time step. Its uncertainty was fixed at 1e-9 ppm/s, which left it effectively frozen.
- It is now an offset in ppm added to the boundary condition CMAQ reads. The CAMS boundary condition is the prior, so the prior offset is 0, and its uncertainty is `BCON_UNC`, by default 0.02 ppm (20 ppb). The offsets are laid out by a boundary map of regions and time steps, stored in the `bcon` group.
- The offsets are always bounded so that the boundary concentration stays non-negative, whatever `ALLOW_NEGATIVE_EMISSIONS` is set to.
- Prior files in the old format are rejected. Regenerate them with `make_prior.py`, and regenerate the templates with `make_template.py`, which now also writes the boundary sensitivity template.

Note: the inversion needs a CMAQ-Adjoint image that writes `ADJ_LGRID_BC`. Posterior emissions differ from earlier releases, since part of the mismatch between model and observations can now be attributed to the boundary condition. See [Boundary conditions](https://github.com/openmethane/openmethane/blob/main/docs/reference/boundary-conditions.md).
