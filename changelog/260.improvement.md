Observation uncertainty now carries the TropOMI aerosol artefact described in [#249](https://github.com/openmethane/openmethane/issues/249). The observed columns fall with retrieved SWIR aerosol while the simulated columns do not, and the dependence grows with the air mass factor, so each sounding's uncertainty gains a term of `103 x air mass factor x AOD` ppb in quadrature with the model uncertainty and the inflated retrieval precision. It downweights the soundings whose light travelled furthest through the most aerosol rather than correcting the column, so it removes no signal. A typical June 2024 sounding over Australia moves from 10.8 to 12.5 ppb, and the inflation ranges over a factor of 1.4 between the middle and the edge of a single swath. Set `OPENMETHANE_AEROSOL_UNCERTAINTY` to `0` for the previous behaviour.
