Convert CMAQ concentrations from a moist-air mixing ratio to the dry-air mole
fraction TROPOMI reports, by folding a per-layer `1 + QV` factor into the
observation operator weights. Previously the model and the retrieval prior were
differenced across two different definitions of "mole fraction", biasing the
simulated column low by roughly 5 ppb over the test domain and more in the
tropics. Observation files gain a `dry_air_factor` field and bump to operator
version 3; existing files must be regenerated.
