Added settings for the resolution of the boundary condition correction: `BCON_SEGMENT_KM`, `BCON_STEP_DAYS` and `BCON_NBANDS` (by default about 1000 km along the boundary, 5 days and 4 bands of equal air mass), and `BCON_REMAINDER` for lengths that don't divide exactly. See [Boundary conditions](https://github.com/openmethane/openmethane/blob/main/docs/reference/boundary-conditions.md).
