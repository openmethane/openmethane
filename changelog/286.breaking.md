Changed the inversion to solve for the initial condition by default, as an offset added to the CAMS initial condition.

- Previously, the initial condition was fixed: `inc_icon` was hard-coded off, and the prior and posterior files had no `icon` group.
- Now one offset per species, in ppm, is added to every cell and layer of the initial condition CMAQ reads on the first day. The CAMS initial condition is the prior, so the prior offset is 0, and its uncertainty is `ICON_UNC`, by default 0.05 ppm (50 ppb). The offset is always bounded so that the initial concentration stays non-negative.
- The prior and posterior files have a new `icon` group holding the offset and its uncertainty. Priors without it are rejected while `INC_ICON` is on. Regenerate them with `make_prior.py`, and rerun `make_template.py`, which now also stages the copy of the initial condition that CMAQ reads.
- To keep the previous behaviour, set `INC_ICON=false`.

Note: posterior emissions differ from earlier releases, since part of the mismatch between model and observations on the first days can now be attributed to the initial condition. See [Boundary conditions](https://github.com/openmethane/openmethane/blob/main/docs/reference/boundary-conditions.md#the-initial-condition).
