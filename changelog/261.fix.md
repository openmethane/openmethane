Interpolate CAMS onto the CMAQ layers instead of giving each layer the value of
the nearest CAMS pressure level, and derive each column's layer pressures from
its own surface pressure. The reconstruction is linear in log pressure and
integrated over each layer's pressure thickness, so the column burden is
preserved. Near the tropopause, where CMAQ's layers are finer than the CAMS
levels, the ICON and BCON layer means move by around 18 ppb.
