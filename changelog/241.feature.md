Added `MPI_EXTRA_ARGS`, passed through to `mpirun` ahead of the CMAQ executable,
so ranks can be bound to cores with `MPI_EXTRA_ARGS="-bind-to core"`. MPICH's
Hydra does not bind by default, leaving ranks free to migrate between cores and
sockets. Binding stays off by default until it has been timed on a production
domain; `docs/reference/performance.md` covers when it helps and when it hurts.
