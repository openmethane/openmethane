Measure the CAMS bias correction with a single forward run instead of
differencing an ICON-only surrogate against a zero-emissions run. The old
decomposition overcorrected by the amount the surrogate failed to reproduce the
forward run, 1.665 ppb of concentration for June 2024, which accounted for the
whole of the inversion's first-guess bias. The correction is now the mean
observed column minus the mean simulated column over every sounding, and it
costs one forward run per month rather than two.
